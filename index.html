About

Nussy Motorsport started in 2026 — not as a business plan, but as a group of sim racers who trusted each other on track and off it. Today we're an international roster racing GT3, GT4 and prototypes on the same simulators real teams use. Next, we take that discipline off the screen: a real kart, in 2027.

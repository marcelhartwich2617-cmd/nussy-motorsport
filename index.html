Pit Board // About

Built like a family. Racing like professionals.

Nussy Motorsport was founded in 2026 — not as a business plan, but as a group of sim racers who trusted each other on track and off it. That trust is still the foundation of everything we do.

Today we're an international roster of 20+ members — drivers, race engineers, strategists, media producers and partnership leads — competing in premier endurance and GT esports championships, on the same simulators used by real-world race teams.

Our next chapter takes that same discipline off the screen: a structured pathway that puts our drivers behind the wheel of a real kart in 2027. Same team. Same green. Real tarmac.
